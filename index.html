Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

1. Who we are

MerchantField Labs ("we", "us") is the developer of the WarrantyDesk application available on the Wix App Market. This policy explains what data WarrantyDesk collects, why, and what rights you have.

2. Data we collect

  • Product registration data: order number, customer email address, product name, serial number and purchase date, submitted by customers on the store's registration page.
  • Warranty claim data: claim description, attached photos or videos, and claim status.
  • Merchant account data: your Wix account identifier and store configuration settings.

3. How we use data

Registration and claim data is used solely to provide the WarrantyDesk service: creating warranty records, processing claims and displaying them in the merchant dashboard. We do not sell personal data. We do not use customer data for advertising.

4. Where data is stored

WarrantyDesk runs on Wix's infrastructure. Data is stored within the store's Wix account. We do not transfer customer data to additional third-party servers.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is retained as long as the merchant's WarrantyDesk subscription is active. When a merchant uninstalls WarrantyDesk, all registration and claim data associated with that store is deleted within 30 days. Merchants and their customers may request earlier deletion by contacting us.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the right to access, correct, export and erase your personal data, and to object to processing. To exercise these rights, contact the store where you registered your product, or reach MerchantField Labs through our listing on the Wix App Market; we respond within 30 days.

7. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to the store where you registered your product, or to MerchantField Labs through our listing on the Wix App Market.
